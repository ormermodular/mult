<p align="center"><b>A chance mult: one input, four outputs, each with its own chance of passing every hit.</b></p>

Mult shares one signal out to four outputs, and every output has its own chance of passing each incoming hit. Feed it a gate or a trigger and each output tosses its own coin: one hit might reach outputs 1 and 4, the next none, the next all of them.

Turn a chance down and that output starts dropping hits, so one clock or drum pattern thins out differently across several voices. At 100% an output is a plain copy of the input, so straight out of the browser it is just a mult. Every choice is fresh and purely random, with no seeds and no evenly spaced modes.

It is polyphonic: each output carries the input's channels, and every channel of every output decides on its own. Four knobs, four lights that flash when a hit passes, and nothing else. 2 HP.

## Installing

Mult is free. Open the [VCV Library](https://library.vcvrack.com/), find **Mult** by Ormer Modular, and subscribe. It appears in Rack's module browser the next time Rack starts. It needs VCV Rack 2 (Free or Pro) on Windows, macOS or Linux.

The manual covers every control and what counts as a hit.
